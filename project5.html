Sweet name--Gulab jamun

  • Indegrients:   dough of khoya (dried milk solids), all-purpose flour, and baking powder, which
                is shaped into balls and deep-fried. The fried balls are soaked in a warm sugar syrup flavored with 
                cardamom, rose water, and saffron.It's typically garnished with chopped nuts before serving.

  • Price:200/kg

Sweet name--Kaju katli

  • Indegrients:  Kaju Katli is made from ground cashew nuts, sugar, and water, cooked into
             a dough. The mixture is rolled out and cut into diamond shapes. Optional ingredients include ghee,
             cardamom powder, and silver leaf for garnish.

  • Price:500/kg

  •

Sweet name--Rasgulla

  • Indegrients: Rasgulla is made from chenna (fresh paneer) and semolina, kneaded into balls and boiled in
             a sugar syrup flavored with cardamom. Optional ingredients include rose water or
             saffron for additional flavor. The spongy balls are soaked in the syrup before serving.

  • Price:250/kg
